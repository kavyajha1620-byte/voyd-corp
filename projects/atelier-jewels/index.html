The atelier

Fewer pieces, made to keep.

Atelier Éclat is arranged around restraint. Instead of a thousand SKUs, a handful of silhouettes that can be worn every day and still feel considered at an event. The site treats product photography and specification with the same seriousness as the brand.

Self-initiated concept project by VOYD CORP. No pieces are for sale and no orders or payments are processed.
