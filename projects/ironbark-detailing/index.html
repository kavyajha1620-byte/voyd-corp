    How it works

    Four stages, done once.

    1. 01

      Inspection

      Paint depth and defect assessment under proper light. You get a written plan, not a guess.

    2. 02

      Decontamination

      Chemical and mechanical decon to strip bonded fallout before any polishing begins.

    3. 03

      Correction

      Multi-stage machine polishing to remove swirls and defects, panel by panel.

    4. 04

      Protection

      Ceramic coating or sealant applied in a controlled bay, then cured and logged.
