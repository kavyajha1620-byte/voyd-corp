    Our story

    A kitchen built
    around one fire.

    Saffron & Stone runs on a single wood-fired hearth. That constraint shapes everything: the menu is short, the timing is honest, and a handful of dishes run out each night because the coals decide, not a spreadsheet.

    Self-initiated concept project by VOYD CORP. This restaurant is fictional and no tables can really be booked.
