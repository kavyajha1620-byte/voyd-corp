How it's made

Slow on purpose,
built to be repaired.

Every piece is made to order in a single workshop. Joints are cut to be taken apart and re-glued, so a table can be re-finished rather than replaced. Lead times are stated as they really are — nothing is "in stock" that isn't.

Self-initiated concept project by VOYD CORP. This maker is fictional and no orders are taken.

  1. 01Timber selected Kiln-dried and checked for movement before cutting.
  2. 02Joints cut Mortise-and-tenon and dovetails, cut by hand and machine.
  3. 03Finished Hard-wax oil or soap, applied in coats and cured.
  4. 04Delivered Blanket-wrapped and assembled in your room.
